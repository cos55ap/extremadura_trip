## 🗓️ 14-Day Detailed Itinerary

### Day 1: El Masnou to Medinaceli & Soria
* **Drive Time:** ~5 hours (470 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Medinaceli (Pueblo Más Bonito):** 2 hours. Roman Triumphal Arch, Plaza Mayor.
  * **Hermitage of San Saturio & Duero Walk (Soria):** 2.5 hours. Historic cliff chapel.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante Casa Augusto* (Soria) – Castilian roast lamb.
  * *La Cervecería Plaza Mayor* (Soria) – Authentic *torreznos de Soria*.
* **Overnight:** **Soria**

---

### Day 2: Soria via Pedraza to Segovia & Ávila
* **Drive Time:** ~3.5 hours (310 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Pedraza (Pueblo Más Bonito):** 1.5 hours. Walled medieval village and castle.
  * **Segovia Aqueduct & Alcázar:** 3 hours.
  * **Ávila Walls:** 2 hours. Ramparts walk.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante José María* (Segovia) – Traditional roasted suckling pig (*cochinillo*).
  * *Restaurante Los Candiles* (Ávila) – *Chuletón de Ávila* (T-bone steak) and *judías del Barco*.
* **Overnight:** **Ávila**

---

### Day 3: Candelario & La Vera Valley
* **Drive Time:** ~2.5 hours (170 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Candelario (Pueblo Más Bonito):** 1.5 hours. Timbered mountain architecture.
  * **Garganta la Olla:** 2 hours. Timber houses and river pools.
  * **Monasterio de San Jerónimo de Yuste:** 1.5 hours. Charles V's retirement palace.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *El Patio De La Posada* (Jarandilla de la Vera) – Traditional Extremaduran meats & *carrillada*.
  * *Casa De Comidas El Labrador* (Jarandilla de la Vera) – Home-cooked regional dishes.
* **Overnight:** **Jarandilla de la Vera** or **Plasencia**

---

### Day 4: Valle del Jerte Canyons & Hikes
* **Drive Time:** ~1 hour local driving
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Garganta de los Infiernos:** 4 hours. Hike to *Los Losares* granite pools.
  * **Garganta de los Papuos:** 2.5 hours. Mountain canyon trail.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante Puerto del Pico* – Mountain stews and grilled meats.
  * *Restaurante Garza Real* (Valdastillas) – Seasonal Jerte dishes and trout.
* **Overnight:** **Jerte** or **Plasencia**

---

### Day 5: Monfragüe National Park & Romangordo
* **Drive Time:** ~1.5 hours (110 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Parque Nacional de Monfragüe:** 4 hours. Birdwatching at *Salto del Gitano*.
  * **Romangordo:** 1.5 hours. Street mural tour.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Carvajal Gastrobar* (Torrejón el Rubio) – Venison, Iberian ham, and wild mushrooms.
  * *Restaurante El Pescador* (Trujillo area) – Classic Extremaduran stews and *migas*.
* **Overnight:** **Trujillo**

---

### Day 6: Trujillo & Guadalupe
* **Drive Time:** ~2 hours round trip (150 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Trujillo:** 3 hours. Plaza Mayor, Pizarro statue, hilltop castle.
  * **Monasterio de Santa María de Guadalupe:** 2 hours. UNESCO monastery tour.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante Bizcocho Plaza* (Trujillo) – Dining on the main square.
  * *Restaurante Posada del Rincón* (Guadalupe) – Venison and *Caldereta de cordero*.
* **Overnight:** **Trujillo**

---

### Day 7: Cáceres & Llanos de Cáceres
* **Drive Time:** ~45 minutes (50 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Cáceres Old Town:** 4 hours. Walled medieval quarter.
  * **Llanos de Cáceres y Sierra de Fuentes:** 2 hours. *Dehesa* landscape drive.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *La Cacharrería* (Cáceres) – Tapas in the historic district.
  * *Restaurante Torre de Sande* (Cáceres) – Dining in a medieval tower courtyard.
* **Overnight:** **Cáceres**

---

### Day 8: Roman Heritage in Mérida
* **Drive Time:** ~50 minutes (75 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Roman Amphitheater & Theater of Mérida:** 2.5 hours.
  * **Roman Bridge & Temple of Diana:** 1.5 hours.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante A de Arco* (Mérida) – Located by Trajan's Arch.
  * *El Puchero de la Nieta* (Mérida) – Traditional *migas extremadunas*.
* **Overnight:** **Mérida**

---

### Day 9: Southern Extremadura – Zafra & Olivenza
* **Drive Time:** ~1.5 hours (120 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Zafra:** 2.5 hours. Plaza Grande & Dukes of Feria Palace.
  * **Olivenza:** 2 hours. Border town with Portuguese Manueline architecture.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante Plaza Grande* (Zafra) – Acorn-fed Iberian pork (*Ibérico de bellota*).
  * *Restaurante Casa Lonja* (Olivenza) – Spanish-Portuguese border cuisine.
* **Overnight:** **Zafra** or **Olivenza**

---

### Day 10: Portuguese Border Fortresses – Elvas & Marvão
* **Drive Time:** ~1.5 hours (110 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Elvas (Portugal):** 3 hours. Star-fortress & Amoreira Aqueduct.
  * **Marvão (Portugal):** 2.5 hours. Mountaintop castle overlooking Alentejo.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Adega Regional O Pimpão* (Elvas) – Classic *Bacalhau Dourado*.
  * *Restaurante O Castelo* (Marvão) – Alentejo acorn pork (*Carne de Porco à Alentejana*).
* **Overnight:** **Marvão** (or **Castelo de Vide**)

---

### Day 11: Sierra de la Estrella High Mountains
* **Drive Time:** ~2 hours (130 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Torre da Estrela Summit:** 1.5 hours.
  * **Manteigas Glacial Valley:** 3 hours. Mountain drives & artisan cheese tasting.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante O Convento* (Manteigas) – River trout and *Queijo da Serra*.
  * *Restaurante Cervejaria Leão* (Covilhã) – Roasted kid goat (*Cabrito assado*).
* **Overnight:** **Manteigas** or **Covilhã**

---

### Day 12: San Martín de Trevejo, Mogarraz & Ciudad Rodrigo
* **Drive Time:** ~2.5 hours (160 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **San Martín de Trevejo (Pueblo Más Bonito):** 1.5 hours. Timbered village with local *A Fala* dialect.
  * **Museo del Aceite "Molino del Medio":** 1 hour. Oil mill museum in Robledillo de Gata.
  * **Mogarraz (Pueblo Más Bonito):** 1.5 hours. Facades with painted resident portraits.
  * **Ciudad Rodrigo:** 2 hours. Walled fortress town.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante Mirador de Mogarraz* (Mogarraz) – Mountain cured meats.
  * *Restaurante Zascandil* (Ciudad Rodrigo) – *Farinato* sausage and regional dishes.
* **Overnight:** **Ciudad Rodrigo**

---

### Day 13: La Alberca to Salamanca
* **Drive Time:** ~1.5 hours (115 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **La Alberca (Pueblo Más Bonito):** 1.5 hours. Timber-framed square in Sierra de Francia.
  * **Salamanca Historic Center:** 4 hours. Plaza Mayor, University, Cathedrals.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante Río de la Plata* (Salamanca) – Classic Castilian roasts.
  * *Tapas Bambú* (Salamanca) – Famous for *Hornazo* meat pie.
* **Overnight:** **Salamanca**

---

### Day 14: Salamanca to El Masnou
* **Drive Time:** ~7.5 hours (770 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Soria River Walk (Return Stop):** 1.5 hours. Leg-stretcher along the Duero.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * *Restaurante Balcón del Duero* (Soria area) – Highway stop for Castilian lunch.
  * *El Racó del Masnou* (El Masnou) – Seafood dinner upon arrival.
* **Overnight:** Home in **El Masnou**
