## 🗓️ 14-Day Detailed Itinerary

### Day 1: El Masnou to Medinaceli ⭐ & Soria
* **Drive Time:** ~5 hours (470 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Medinaceli ⭐](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Medinaceli+Pueblo+Mas+Bonito+Spain):** 2 hours. Roman Triumphal Arch, Plaza Mayor.
  * **[Hermitage of San Saturio](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ermita+de+San+Saturio+Soria) & Duero Walk (Soria):** 2.5 hours. Historic cliff chapel.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [Restaurante Casa Augusto](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Casa+Augusto+Soria) (Soria) – Castilian roast lamb.
  * [La Cervecería Plaza Mayor](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=La+Cerveceria+Plaza+Mayor+Soria) (Soria) – Authentic *torreznos de Soria*.
* **Overnight:** **Soria**

---

### Day 2: Soria via Pedraza ⭐ to Segovia & Ávila
* **Drive Time:** ~3.5 hours (310 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Pedraza ⭐](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pedraza+Segovia):** 1.5 hours. Walled medieval village and castle.
  * **[Segovia Aqueduct & Alcázar](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Alcazar+de+Segovia):** 3 hours.
  * **[Ávila Walls](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Muralla+de+Avila):** 2 hours. Ramparts walk.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [Restaurante José María](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Jose+Maria+Segovia) (Segovia) – Traditional roasted suckling pig (*cochinillo*).
  * [Restaurante Los Candiles](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Los+Candiles+Avila) (Ávila) – *Chuletón de Ávila* (T-bone steak) and *judías del Barco*.
* **Overnight:** **Ávila**

---

### Day 3: Candelario ⭐ & La Vera Valley
* **Drive Time:** ~2.5 hours (170 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Candelario ⭐](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Candelario+Salamanca):** 1.5 hours. Timbered mountain architecture.
  * **[Garganta la Olla ⭐](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Garganta+la+Olla+Caceres):** 2 hours. Timber houses and river pools.
  * **[Monasterio de San Jerónimo de Yuste](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Monasterio+de+San+Jeronimo+de+Yuste):** 1.5 hours. Charles V's retirement palace.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [El Patio De La Posada](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=El+Patio+De+La+Posada+Jarandilla+de+la+Vera) (Jarandilla de la Vera) – Traditional Extremaduran meats & *carrillada*.
  * [Casa De Comidas El Labrador](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Casa+De+Comidas+El+Labrador+Jarandilla+de+la+Vera) (Jarandilla de la Vera) – Home-cooked regional dishes.
* **Overnight:** **Jarandilla de la Vera** or **Plasencia**

---

### Day 4: Valle del Jerte Canyons & Hikes
* **Drive Time:** ~1 hour local driving
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Garganta de los Infiernos](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Reserva+Natural+Garganta+de+los+Infiernos):** 4 hours. Hike to *Los Losares* granite pools.
  * **[Garganta de los Papuos](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Garganta+de+los+Papuos+Jerte):** 2.5 hours. Mountain canyon trail.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [Restaurante Puerto del Pico](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Puerto+del+Pico+Avila) – Mountain stews and grilled meats.
  * [Restaurante Garza Real](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Garza+Real+Valdastillas) (Valdastillas) – Seasonal Jerte dishes and trout.
* **Overnight:** **Jerte** or **Plasencia**

---

### Day 5: Monfragüe National Park & Romangordo
* **Drive Time:** ~1.5 hours (110 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Parque Nacional de Monfragüe](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Parque+Nacional+de+Monfrague):** 4 hours. Birdwatching at *Salto del Gitano*.
  * **[Romangordo](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Romangordo+Caceres):** 1.5 hours. Street mural tour.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [Carvajal Gastrobar](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Carvajal+Gastrobar+Torrejon+el+Rubio) (Torrejón el Rubio) – Venison, Iberian ham, and wild mushrooms.
  * [Restaurante El Pescador](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+El+Pescador+Trujillo) (Trujillo area) – Classic Extremaduran stews and *migas*.
* **Overnight:** **Trujillo**

---

### Day 6: Trujillo & Guadalupe ⭐
* **Drive Time:** ~2 hours round trip (150 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Trujillo Historic Center](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Castillo+de+Trujillo):** 3 hours. Plaza Mayor, Pizarro statue, hilltop castle.
  * **[Monasterio de Santa María de Guadalupe ⭐](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Real+Monasterio+de+Santa+Maria+de+Guadalupe):** 2 hours. UNESCO monastery tour.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [Restaurante Bizcocho Plaza](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Bizcocho+Plaza+Trujillo) (Trujillo) – Dining on the main square.
  * [Restaurante Posada del Rincón](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Posada+del+Rincon+Guadalupe) (Guadalupe ⭐) – Venison and *Caldereta de cordero*.
* **Overnight:** **Trujillo**

---

### Day 7: Cáceres & Llanos de Cáceres
* **Drive Time:** ~45 minutes (50 km)

* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [La Cacharrería](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=La+Cacharreria+Caceres) (Cáceres) – Tapas in the historic district.
  * [Restaurante Torre de Sande](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Torre+de+Sande+Caceres) (Cáceres) – Dining in a medieval tower courtyard.
* **Overnight:** **Cáceres**

### Day 8: Roman Heritage in Mérida
* **Drive Time:** ~50 minutes (75 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Teatro y Anfiteatro Romano de Mérida](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Teatro+Romano+de+Merida):** 2.5 hours.
  * **[Puente Romano & Templo de Diana](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Templo+de+Diana+Merida):** 1.5 hours.

### Day 9: Southern Extremadura – Zafra & Olivenza
* **Drive Time:** ~1.5 hours (120 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Zafra](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Zafra+Badajoz):** 2.5 hours. Plaza Grande & Dukes of Feria Palace.
  * **[Olivenza](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Olivenza+Badajoz):** 2 hours. Border town with Portuguese Manueline architecture.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [Restaurante Plaza Grande](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Plaza+Grande+Zafra) (Zafra) – Acorn-fed Iberian pork (*Ibérico de bellota*).
  * [Restaurante Casa Lonja](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Casa+Lonja+Olivenza) (Olivenza) – Spanish-Portuguese border cuisine.
* **Overnight:** **Zafra** or **Olivenza**

### Day 10: Portuguese Border Fortresses – Elvas & Marvão
* **Drive Time:** ~1.5 hours (110 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**

* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [Adega Regional O Pimpão](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Adega+Regional+O+Pimpao+Elvas) (Elvas) – Classic *Bacalhau Dourado*.
