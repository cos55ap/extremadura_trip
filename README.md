## 🗓️ 14-Day Detailed Itinerary

### Day 1: El Masnou to Medinaceli ⭐ & Soria
* **Drive Time:** ~5 hours (470 km)
* **EV Charging Pinpoints (>200 km segment):**
  * **Primary (Endesa X Way / Enel X):** [Endesa X Way Charging Hub (CC Plaza Imperial, Zaragoza)](https://maps.google.com/?q=41.6371747,-0.9047503)
  * **Backup (Tesla / Zunder):** [Tesla Supercharger Zaragoza West / El Cisne](https://maps.google.com/?q=41.624700,-1.008986)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Medinaceli ⭐](https://maps.google.com/?q=41.1717,-2.4339):** 2 hours. Roman Triumphal Arch, Plaza Mayor.
  * **[Hermitage of San Saturio](https://maps.google.com/?q=41.7583,-2.4578) & Duero Walk (Soria):** 2.5 hours. Historic cliff chapel.
* **Local Restaurants:**
  * [Restaurante Casa Augusto](https://maps.google.com/?q=41.7644,-2.4642) (Soria) – Castilian roast lamb.
  * [La Cervecería Plaza Mayor](https://maps.google.com/?q=41.7631,-2.4651) (Soria) – Authentic *torreznos de Soria*.
* **Overnight:** **Soria**

---

### Day 2: Soria via Pedraza ⭐ to Segovia & Ávila
* **Drive Time:** ~3.5 hours (310 km)
* **EV Charging Pinpoints (>200 km segment):**
  * **Primary (Endesa X Way / Iberdrola):** [Endesa X Way Charging Hub (CC Luz de Castilla, Segovia)](https://maps.google.com/?q=40.931221,-4.116345)
  * **Backup (Tesla / Zunder):** [Tesla Supercharger Ávila (Hotel Cuatro Postes / Commercial Zone)](https://maps.google.com/?q=40.660602,-4.706341)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **[Pedraza ⭐](https://maps.google.com/?q=41.1303,-3.8117):** 1.5 hours. Walled medieval village and castle.
  * **[Segovia Aqueduct & Alcázar](https://maps.google.com/?q=40.9481,-4.1184):** 3 hours.
  * **[Ávila Walls](https://maps.google.com/?q=40.6565,-4.6818):** 2 hours. Ramparts walk.
* **Local Restaurants:**

  * [Restaurante Los Candiles](https://maps.google.com/?q=40.6559,-4.6983) (Ávila) – *Chuletón de Ávila* (T-bone steak) and *judías del Barco*.
