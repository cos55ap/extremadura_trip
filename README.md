## 🗓️ 14-Day Detailed Itinerary

### Day 1: El Masnou to Medinaceli & Soria
* **Drive Time:** ~5 hours (470 km)
* **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
  * **Medinaceli (Pueblo Más Bonito):** 2 hours. Roman Triumphal Arch, Plaza Mayor.
    * **Hermitage of San Saturio & Duero Walk (Soria):** 2.5 hours. Historic cliff chapel[span_58](start_span)[span_58](end_span).
    * **Local Restaurants:**
      * *Restaurante Casa Augusto* (Soria) – Castilian roast lamb.
        * *La Cervecería Plaza Mayor* (Soria) – Authentic *torreznos de Soria*.
        * **Overnight:** **Soria**[span_59](start_span)[span_59](end_span)

        ---

        ### Day 2: Soria via Pedraza to Segovia & Ávila
        * **Drive Time:** ~3.5 hours (310 km)
        * **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
          * **Pedraza (Pueblo Más Bonito):** 1.5 hours. Walled medieval village and castle.
            * **Segovia Aqueduct & Alcázar:** 3 hours[span_60](start_span)[span_60](end_span).
              * **Ávila Walls:** 2 hours. Ramparts walk[span_61](start_span)[span_61](end_span).
              * **Local Restaurants:**
                * *Restaurante José María* (Segovia) – Traditional roasted suckling pig (*cochinillo*).
                  * *Restaurante Los Candiles* (Ávila) – *Chuletón de Ávila* (T-bone steak) and *judías del Barco*.
                  * **Overnight:** **Ávila**[span_62](start_span)[span_62](end_span)

                  ---

                  ### Day 3: Candelario & La Vera Valley
                  * **Drive Time:** ~2.5 hours (170 km)
                  * **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
                    * **Candelario (Pueblo Más Bonito):** 1.5 hours. Timbered mountain architecture.
                      * **Garganta la Olla:** 2 hours. Timber houses and river pools[span_63](start_span)[span_63](end_span).
                        * **Monasterio de San Jerónimo de Yuste:** 1.5 hours. Charles V's retirement palace[span_64](start_span)[span_64](end_span).
                        * **Local Restaurants:**
                          * *El Patio De La Posada* (Jarandilla de la Vera) – Traditional Extremaduran meats & *carrillada*[span_65](start_span)[span_65](end_span).
                            * *Casa De Comidas El Labrador* (Jarandilla de la Vera) – Home-cooked regional dishes.
                            * **Overnight:** **Jarandilla de la Vera** or **Plasencia**[span_66](start_span)[span_66](end_span)

                            ---

                            ### Day 4: Valle del Jerte Canyons & Hikes
                            * **Drive Time:** ~1 hour local driving
                            * **Attractions & Time On-Site:**
                              * **Garganta de los Infiernos:** 4 hours. Hike to *Los Losares* granite pools[span_67](start_span)[span_67](end_span).
